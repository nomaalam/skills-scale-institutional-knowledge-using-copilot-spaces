# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers represent user needs and turn research insights into usable, accessible experience requirements and designs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, usability testing, and discovery activities
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and experience guidelines
- Document usability and accessibility requirements
- Validate proposed solutions against user needs and research findings
- Identify experience risks and recommend mitigations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to refine problem statements, personas, and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to balance experience goals with technical constraints
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility scenarios
- Keep Project Managers informed about research dependencies, decisions, and delivery risks

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, architecture decisions, engineering standards, and technical risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Define and document architecture, integration, and implementation approaches
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks
- Establish engineering standards and review significant technical decisions
- Support estimation and decomposition of complex work
- Ensure solutions remain maintainable, secure, observable, and scalable

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to track dependencies, risks, and technical milestones
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and scope trade-offs
- Guide Developers through design reviews and implementation decisions
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability and non-functional quality requirements

---

## DevOps and Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Site Reliability Engineers make delivery and operation of the product repeatable, observable, and reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines, environments, configuration, and release automation
- Define observability, alerting, capacity, and reliability requirements
- Verify operational readiness and rollback or mitigation plans
- Support incident response and post-incident improvement work
- Track infrastructure and operational risks through release and delivery planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to automate delivery and improve system reliability
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on staging environments, smoke tests, and release verification
- Partner with Project Managers on deployment dependencies, operational risks, and incident timelines
- Advise Product Managers and Stakeholders about operational impact and release readiness

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads ensure that project decisions and delivered solutions address security, privacy, and compliance obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements during planning
- Facilitate threat modeling, security reviews, and data protection assessments
- Define security acceptance criteria and required controls
- Track vulnerabilities, findings, and remediation commitments
- Advise on secure design, handling of sensitive data, and incident readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to prioritize security and privacy work
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers on secure architecture and implementation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on security test coverage and release gates
- Escalate unresolved risks to Stakeholders or the Business Sponsor when they affect delivery decisions

---

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads make sure teams can measure whether delivered work achieves its intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation, event, dashboard, and reporting requirements
- Establish data quality checks and metric definitions
- Validate baseline measurements and post-release results
- Help teams interpret outcomes and identify opportunities for iteration
- Document data dependencies, assumptions, and limitations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to connect success metrics to product decisions
- Collaborate with Developers and Technical Leads on reliable instrumentation
- Partner with QA/Testing to verify event tracking and reporting behavior
- Provide Project Managers and Stakeholders with evidence for status reviews and outcome decisions

---

## Customer Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and service-readiness needs into delivery planning and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Identify support workflows, operational procedures, and customer communication needs
- Review features for supportability and readiness before release
- Prepare or coordinate knowledge-base content, training, and service announcements
- Report customer-impacting issues, recurring problems, and operational feedback
- Participate in incident communications and post-incident improvement work

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to represent customer needs and prioritize service improvements
- Coordinate with Project Managers on readiness checklists, communications, and escalations
- Work with Developers, QA/Testing, and DevOps/SRE on reproducing and resolving customer-impacting issues
- Provide Stakeholders with feedback on adoption, support volume, and customer impact

---

## Business Sponsors and Executive Approvers

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors and Executive Approvers provide strategic sponsorship, confirm investment and priority, and make decisions that exceed the delivery team’s authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business outcome, funding, and strategic priority of the initiative
- Approve major scope, timeline, risk, and release decisions
- Resolve escalated cross-functional conflicts and resource constraints
- Remove organizational blockers and support stakeholder alignment
- Review outcome metrics and decide whether to continue, change, or close the initiative

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receive recommendations and escalation summaries from Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on outcomes, roadmap priorities, and trade-offs
- Rely on Technical Leads, Security and Privacy Leads, and other specialists for risk-based decisions
- Provide direction to Stakeholders and confirm decisions at initiation, release, and escalation gates
