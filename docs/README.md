# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection explains how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and improves projects. The process emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, documented risks and dependencies, quality checks, stakeholder communication, and continuous improvement.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme begins with **initiation**, validating the business need, defining measurable goals and success criteria, identifying stakeholders, outlining milestones and risks, and deciding whether to proceed to planning. During **planning**, approved initiatives become prioritized and estimated backlog items with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, dependencies, risks, and a release plan.

During **execution and tracking**, teams deliver small increments through a project board, regular standups, delivery syncs, demos, pull-request review, automated checks, and QA. **Risk and communication management** uses a maintained risk register, dependency monitoring, regular stakeholder updates, and defined escalation paths for blockers and business-impacting issues.

For **release and deployment**, teams confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, rollback or mitigation plans, staging smoke tests, production verification, and stakeholder communication. After sprints, releases, or incidents, **retrospectives and continuous improvement** capture what went well, identify improvements, and track a small number of owned, time-bound action items.

Clear roles support the lifecycle: Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work; Developers implement and test solutions; QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
